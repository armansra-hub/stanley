# Customer criteria publication

Explore Jev Intelligence → Find operating matches → Similar to recent customers uses the selected private customer dictionary. The authored package has 112 complete criteria, organized into 12 browsing families, and 35 provider-industry definitions. A family is navigation, not an automatic broad match. An evidence field is not a positive criterion.

Choose All customer patterns, or choose an industry to use All customer patterns within industry. A specific criterion shows its entire predicate, required evidence, exclusions and attributable customer examples. Mixed businesses can appear in several industries. The industry describes what the provider does, not the industries it sells to. Missing industry evidence is explicit.

The customer side uses authored website research, not a paid Jev classification. The private package accounts for 823 maintained announcement records; this is not necessarily 823 distinct legal companies. Available business scopes and unresolved identities remain separate. Full-site archive completion is never fabricated from completion of the narrower approved business scope. Customer status comes from Ring Ring; identity or website gaps do not revoke that status.

Prospect matches require saved Jev support for the complete criterion and an authored customer binding for that same criterion. A shared keyword, client industry, or partial clause is insufficient. Both sides retain source passages and scope. Asset-based operations and non-asset freight arrangement have distinct definitions; a brokerage division cannot label its whole fleet-owning group non-asset. Operating resemblance does not establish buying intent or financial pain.

Names/domains support quick copy. Records open in the existing account panel. Selection, dismissal and Show hidden reuse existing review behavior. Legacy saved matches remain in their own labeled view. A new criterion with no evaluation says not yet evaluated, rather than implying no prospects qualify.

## Runtime and cost

The existing `account_catalog` lane loads the immutable dictionary named by its work lease. Its native requests include each criterion's full definition, exclusions, examples and ownership/time rules. The 35 source-backed industry questions supply context; uncertain industry never silently drops a criterion. There is no second semantic judge.

Requests retain their existing size bounds and exact-request reuse. Criterion versions depend on their meaning and model contract, not customer display changes or unrelated definitions. Unchanged compatible evidence/answers can therefore be reused. Native answers, unknowns, conflicts, source hashes and request receipts remain intact. Earlier versions remain readable in history. New definitions are not claimed as already evaluated merely because a legacy answer exists.

Paid purposes are restricted to useful public trigger interpretation and prospect operating classification. Jev does not grade TAM accounts, research or reclassify the customer reference corpus, rank research URLs, or answer arbitrary paid saved queries under this policy. The existing source collectors and saved results remain available.

Registration and selection are free database operations. Neither changes the paid enable switch, admits new catalog work, or sends a provider request. Resume is a separate user-authorized operation through the existing intelligence controls; funding the provider by itself does not remove Stanley's explicit pause. After resumption, the existing workers service the selected dictionary against the canonical prospect set, preserving source gaps and safe exact-request continuation. There is no new scheduler or parallel TAM queue.
