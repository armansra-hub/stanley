# Federal recipient identity expansion

Stanley compares the lead's stored name, website, legal aliases and sourced addresses with government recipients. SAM's second address line is retained, including its source and the street it belongs to. Names that differ by useful abbreviations, spacing or ordering become research candidates; those similarities never establish a match on their own.

Exact verified government identifiers and supported deterministic matches stay free. For uncertain recipients, the existing Jev integration interprets already collected official company declarations, contact/location evidence and government identity fields. Private CRM address lines become comparison facts; they are not copied into the public response cache. Jev's original answer and probabilities are retained without a second model judging them or a numerical confidence cutoff.

Jev can identify the same business, a related company, a different business or insufficient evidence. Parent/subsidiary/joint-venture evidence stays separate from direct contract totals. Existing explicit rejected bindings are preserved. Weak historical links use the existing leased repair process, and pending recipients use that same worker and audit receipts. This does not restart CRM evidence refresh or TAM grading.

## Cost and continuation behavior

- Reuse keys include material names, domains, identifiers, addresses and relevant evidence. They exclude collection time, observation IDs and individual award IDs/URLs. Repeated same-site structured identity facts are deduplicated while retaining source provenance.
- No usable identity evidence means no paid inference. That result remains unresolved, rather than claiming no government business.
- Existing Jev budgets, request locking and exact-answer reuse apply. Deferred or uncertain provider calls keep the exact candidate checkpoint.
- Discovery handles distinct plausible recipients individually, preserving remaining candidates, pages, aliases and contract-vehicle searches after its first successful match. The award-history name query also remains open to additional legitimate recipients.
- Existing cloud source jobs perform the work. No extra Codex automation, grading queue, scheduler or outreach was added.

## In the lead record

Open the lead's federal/account intelligence section. Direct recipients, unresolved candidates and sourced related-company activity remain separate. Federal source cards distinguish unfinished searches, failed queries, identity evidence still needed and completed searches with no verified match. Expand the saved recipient decision to inspect original Jev data when present.

“Complete” describes the saved source/query/date scope, not universal coverage of every government contract. Source omissions, unavailable evidence and provider result-window limits remain visible. Tests validate matching and continuation cases; they do not establish a measured real-world zero-error rate or a guaranteed savings percentage.
