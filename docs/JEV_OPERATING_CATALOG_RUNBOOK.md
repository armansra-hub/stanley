# Operating intelligence rollout

This release implements 47 prospecting categories, 35 industry guides, eight
cross-industry lessons and ten local combinations from the September 24 research.
The user removed proposed category O06 (customer renewal/expansion). The original
research remains historical evidence; its customer names and Slack excerpts are
not shipped in client JavaScript or sent in routine model requests.

## Boundaries

- This is public prospecting intelligence. Jev does not perform TAM grading.
  The private grading endpoint and private connector requests are disabled.
- No second semantic model judges Jev. Native choices and optional probabilities
  are retained unchanged. Old 22-topic probability policies remain separate.
- Native questions distinguish target/customer/partner roles, owned/arranged
  assets, brands/legal entities, explicit dates, unknowns and discovery hypotheses.
  They do not assert finance pain or intent from a business model.
- Installing this release does not fund or enable paid work, admit the TAM,
  delete old jobs, create a grading queue, or add a scheduler.

## How coverage works

The existing directed-research account lease owns the work. First inventory and
reuse retained current evidence. Pack the shared state and unanswered questions
by actual byte/question limits. For large corpora, retain complete source packets,
map relevant/partial/conflicting material with native questions, then answer the
complete predicate across those sources. Never truncate an oversized predicate's
corpus to manufacture a complete answer; retain it as a visible source gap.

Each paid response and request fingerprint is checkpointed immediately. Resume
the exact pending request before another one. A source/hash or semantic version
change invalidates the affected coverage; capture timestamps alone do not.
Research follows missing branches through the existing source/query frontier.
Repeated unknowns over unchanged evidence do not justify repeating paid answers.

Stored source references are shared across facets where identical. UI citations
remain tied to exact company, source hash and UTF-16 offsets. Database readback
requires current semantic versions; stale answers cannot acquire new labels.

`complete` means the current 47 questions were evaluated over retained evidence.
It does not mean every answer is positive, every source is accessible, or all
possible research is exhausted. Pending, partial and source/budget gaps remain
visible separately. The older “interpreted sources” count does not certify new
catalog coverage. Operating answers do not complete unrelated event, identity or
custom-question jobs.

## UI

Open **Explore Jev Intelligence → Find operating matches**. Categories are grouped
by industry/model, with definitions, counts, a filter and ten research combinations.
Any/All searches can combine old and new traits. The reviewed combinations retain
their exact AND/OR branches and are candidate lists, not claims of confirmed pain.

Expand an account's result to read source context, the category boundary,
unverified discovery hypothesis and raw Jev answer. Company/domain copying,
in-record account intelligence and dismiss/restore behavior remain available.
The budget panel shows current policy amounts separately from historical monthly
accounting. Browsing and refreshing saved results do not dispatch Jev.

## Recovery

Read the exact receipt and current state before retrying uncertain writes. A
stored successful native response is reused, even if its original accounting
settlement needs repair. A pre-dispatch failure can release its reservation only
when the protocol proves that no dispatch was authorized. Never recycle uncertain
spend by clearing history. Pause controls do not hide cached results.

Do not restore old CRM-refresh automations, edit TAM grading workers, purchase
credits, enable auto-recharge or send outreach as part of this rollout.
