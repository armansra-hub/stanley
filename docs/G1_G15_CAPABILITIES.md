# G1–G15 capability map

This is an implementation inventory for Stanley's NetSuite prospecting work, dated September 20, 2026. **It is not a deployment receipt.** Applied migrations, deployed revisions, enabled schedules and live completion receipts establish operational status separately. G1–G15 refer to the implementation audit's gap numbers; G6 here is government announcements, not the excluded original outreach workstream 6.

Open **Intelligence** at `/headhunter/intelligence` for operating matches, saved research questions, evidence, activity and cost. From a lead record, **Account intelligence →** opens that account's research, operating profile, source passages, native Jev answers, hiring context and related accounts. The lead record also contains federal coverage, **Contract timing and official records**, and **Regional government contracts**. Eligible dated developments appear in **Triggered**; stored operating evidence can be useful without producing a trigger.

## Jev connection for Codex

The [stdio connector](../tools/jev_codex_mcp.py) exposes `jev_status`, `jev_evaluate` and `jev_account_context` through Stanley's authenticated agent endpoints. Once registered in the Codex host, it can ask bounded typed questions over supplied context and return native Jev answers and usage, or retrieve an exact NetSuite account's stored public intelligence. It does not browse on its own or change TAM grades.

Evaluation uses the existing direct TypeSafe connection and Stanley budget controls. Private excerpts are the default input classification; public caching requires public input. Local receipts preserve completed results and uncertain submissions are not blindly repeated. The local Codex host has the `stanley_jev` MCP entry installed. A live Jev 1.13.0 invocation and exact-repeat local reuse were verified on September 20, 2026. Open a fresh Codex task/tool session to load the entry; the running grading task does not need to restart.

## Operating boundaries

New capabilities reuse durable source state, cached paid answers, leases, deadlines and spend reservations. Configured five-, ten- and fifteen-minute workers process due work; those intervals do not promise complete TAM rotation or real-time source-to-card latency. Source backoff, provider availability, backlog and the budget determine actual progress.

The fifteen-minute changed-evidence monitor remains active, and the sole canonical TAM owner continues bulk grading. A validation hold is a supported record outcome: unresolved evidence preserves the prior grade and its hold reason instead of forcing a replacement publication. The September 20 11:14:09 UTC readback confirmed that outcome for ID 10530959, with its fresh PDF/seed bound and zero successor publications. Twenty-four SQL fixture checks separately passed at 11:15 UTC, including publication against matching evidence hashes and rejection of a wrong hash. G15 activation does not imply every changed record can immediately receive a new grade.

The focus remains business-services NetSuite prospecting. Full native Jev answers and source passages remain inspectable without an added semantic reviewer; publication is a separate application action. The existing canonical TAM grader retains its independent full-record validator. Public signals never change TAM or Old Gold grades. Outreach sending, contact/persona campaigns, job applications and deal-folder automation remain outside this build.

This inventory does not establish all-TAM coverage, complete federal histories, proven factual accuracy, improved lead yield, or a sustainable monthly cost. Use live coverage, source-health, queue, spend and publication receipts to measure those outcomes.
