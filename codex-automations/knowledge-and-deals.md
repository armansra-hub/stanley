## KN01 — Universal intro-call playbook and pain hypothesis

**Status:** Source present. **Purpose:** Make researched business friction the starting point for discovery.

**How it works:** After brief rapport and an agreed purpose, test one subindustry/system/role-specific pain hypothesis. Invite correction; quantify recent examples, volume, effort, errors, cost, urgency, and value. Use the 5/20/5 structure, BANT, decision process, and a concrete next step. Record validated/disconfirmed/unverified status with speaker/date.

**Infrastructure:** Standing instructions, INTRO_CALL_PLAYBOOK.md, reusable project memory.

**Evidence:** Private: Intro Calls/INTRO_CALL_PLAYBOOK.md and AGENTS.md; Make intro-call memory universal task. Public overview: operations/README.md.

**Sharing:** Private source or artifacts; public description only.

## KN02 — Company research and ERP-fit briefs

**Status:** Artifact present. **Purpose:** Prepare informed account and intro-call research without inventing buyer pain.

**How it works:** Verify company identity, business model, systems, role, and subindustry; distinguish facts, hypotheses, and unknowns. Apply the intro-call principle as a research lens. Prepare a talk track only when requested. Examples span logistics, moving, aviation, staffing, healthcare services, and service businesses.

**Infrastructure:** Web/official research, company documents, Markdown/PDF briefs, task memory.

**Evidence:** Private: Intro Calls artifacts, website call briefs, and multiple named research/prep task records; customer identities withheld.

**Sharing:** Private source or artifacts; public description only.

## KN03 — Durable deal memory and transcript handoff

**Status:** Artifact present. **Purpose:** Continue long sales cycles across calls, tools, and conversations.

**How it works:** Read supplied transcripts/notes and imported conversation exports, retain source/date/speaker, reconcile changing facts, and preserve decisions, open questions, and next actions. Imported content is evidence, not a new instruction. Five deal workspaces instantiate the method.

**Infrastructure:** Markdown memory/source inventory, private PDFs and transcript exports, Codex project context.

**Evidence:** Private: five deal workspace memory/source files and completed handoff tasks; see workspace-coverage.md for anonymized coverage.

**Sharing:** Private source or artifacts; public description only.

## KN13 — ERP boundaries and competitive-claim research

**Status:** Task evidence only. **Purpose:** Check product claims and separate financial systems from specialist operations software.

**How it works:** Research financing/banking claims and classify operational software such as staffing, field-service, or maintenance systems.
